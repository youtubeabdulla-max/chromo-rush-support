Chromo Rush — Support

Updated 25 October 2026 · Chromo Rush 2.1.0

Need help with Chromo Rush, a purchase or an ad? E-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com, or use Settings → Privacy & legal → Email support in the game (the e-mail then names your game version). We answer within 2 working days.

Chromo Rush has no accounts and no server of its own, so there is no online profile to delete. Settings → Privacy & legal → Reset all data erases the game's data from your device (what you bought comes back by itself, or with Restore purchases). What the ad partners, Unity, Apple and Google keep is explained in the Privacy Policy, with how to reach them. Chromo Rush is made by Abdulla Alhashmi.

Playing

  • PLAY on Home starts your next stage of the Sky Path: 48 stages in 6 skies, up to 3 stars each (FLAG, 1ST, NO OOPS). Clear a sky's Summit and collect enough stars to open the next sky. MAP shows every stage; tap one to race it again.
  • RIVAL RACE on Home is a quick race against three rivals on your racer's own sky.
  • RAINBOW: every race paints your rainbow bar; each new rainbow up to rainbow 30 gives a free trail or trim. Tap the bar on Home to see every prize and your sticker album (a sticker for each stage with all 3 stars).
  • Gift balloon: on each new day you play, a balloon waits by your ball on Home. Nothing is lost on days you don't play.
  • Settings has Large text, Reduced effects, Obstacle captions and the Rival Race difficulty.

Purchases

Chromo Rush is free to play. In Skins (tap your ball on Home) you can buy racers one by one, ALL 5 RACERS together, SPECIAL TRAILS (six trails behind your ball) and No ads. They change only the look, never how a race plays, and each is a one-time purchase. The App Store or Google Play takes the payment and shows the price before you buy.

  • Restore purchases: in Skins or in Settings. It brings back what you bought with the same Apple ID or Google account, for example after a reinstall, after Reset all data or on a new phone. The game also checks with the store by itself each time it starts, and again later if the store could not answer.
  • WAITING: with Ask to Buy or a pending payment, the item's button says WAITING; the item arrives once the purchase is approved. While a parent decides (a day at most), the game does not offer the same thing again.
  • "Store not reachable": the game could not reach the App Store or Google Play (no internet, or no Google account in the Play Store app). Tap TRY AGAIN, or open Skins again later.
  • Refunds: Apple and Google handle all payments, so ask them: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through your Google Play order history or Google Play Help. We cannot refund purchases ourselves.
  • Free things live on your phone: rainbow prizes, stickers, tries and what the update from version 1.x gave you (the thank-you skin Prism, and the skins and No ads it gave you for what you had in version 1.x) are not store purchases: Reset all data or deleting the game removes them, and Restore purchases cannot bring them back.

Ads

Chromo Rush shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, formerly ironSource) from our ad partners Unity Ads and Google AdMob. A short ad can come between races, never during one: on the Sky Path once stage 3 has been raced to its flag, when you start a stage, at least 60 seconds after the last one (never at a Summit, after a new obstacle's sheet or from the new-sky card); in Rival Race after a race that ran to its end, when you start the next one, at least 45 seconds after the previous one. None comes when you open the game, after a race in which a Continue video was shown, or once you own No ads. The Continue video (Rival Race) and the TRY video (Skins: race three times as a racer you don't own) play only when you tap them. If an ad does not load, just carry on playing; there is nothing to fix.

  • Personalised ads (13 and over): change your answer at any time with the Personalised ads switch in Settings → Privacy & legal. Either answer shows the same number of ads. With No thanks (switch off) the game sends the ad partners both the US "do not sell or share" opt-out signal and the European "no consent" signal, so the ads you see are not personalised.
  • Tracking on iPhone (13 and over): iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: delete or reset it in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • No ads: a one-time purchase in Skins that turns off the ads between races; the Continue and TRY videos stay optional. After a reinstall, Reset all data or on a new phone, use Restore purchases in Skins or Settings.
  • Report an ad: Settings → Privacy & legal → Report an ad opens your e-mail app with a report to us that already contains the ad network, the ad's creative id and the time of the last ad shown since you opened the game. Add what you saw and send it. You can also simply e-mail us with the time and, if you can, a screenshot.
  • A Continue or TRY video gave no reward? E-mail us with the time.

For parents

  • Chromo Rush is for all ages. The first time it is opened it asks HOW OLD ARE YOU?; only the band (under 13, or 13 and over) is kept, on the device. Under 13, every ad is child-directed and never personalised, and the game never asks to allow tracking (Privacy Policy: Your age and children).
  • There is no chat, no player names, no account and no notifications. No purchase or prize is random, nothing is bought with an in-game currency, and no ad or purchase gives stars or prizes.
  • Every purchase needs the store's own confirmation, and Ask to Buy or Google Play purchase approvals apply. Reset all data asks the age question again.

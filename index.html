Chromo Rush — Support

Updated 19 October 2026 · Chromo Rush 2.0.0

Need help with Chromo Rush, a purchase or an ad? E-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com, or use Settings → Privacy & legal → Email support in the game (the e-mail then names your game version). We answer within 2 working days.

Chromo Rush has no accounts and no server of its own, so there is no online profile to delete. Settings → Privacy & legal → Reset all data erases the game's data from your device (what you bought comes back with Restore purchases). What the ad partners, Unity, Apple and Google keep is explained in the Privacy Policy, with how to reach them. Chromo Rush is made by Abdulla Alhashmi.

Purchases

Chromo Rush is free to play. Skins (they change only the look, never how a race plays) and No ads are one-time purchases in Skins (the palette icon). The App Store or Google Play takes the payment and shows the price before you buy.

  • Restore purchases: in Skins or in Settings. It brings back what you bought with the same Apple ID or Google account, for example after a reinstall, after Reset all data or on a new phone. The game also checks with the store by itself each time it starts, and again later if the store could not answer.
  • Waiting for approval: with Ask to Buy or a pending payment, the item's button says WAITING; the item arrives once the purchase is approved.
  • Refunds: Apple and Google handle all payments, so ask them: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through your Google Play order history or Google Play Help. We cannot refund purchases ourselves.
  • What the update to 2.0 gave you (the thank-you skin Prism, and the skins and No ads it gave you for what you had in version 1.x) lives only on the phone where the update happened: Reset all data or deleting the game removes it, and Restore purchases cannot bring it back, because it was never a store purchase.

Ads

Chromo Rush shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, formerly ironSource) from our ad partners Unity Ads and Google AdMob: the optional Continue video, which plays only when you tap Continue after your ball bursts, and a short ad between races. That short ad can come after a race that ran to its end (not one you quit), when you start your next race, at least 45 seconds after the previous one. It never comes during a race, when you open the game, after a race in which a Continue video was shown, or once you own No ads. If an ad does not load, just carry on playing; there is nothing to fix.

  • Personalised ads: change your answer at any time with the Personalised ads switch in Settings → Privacy & legal. Either answer shows the same number of ads. With No thanks (switch off) the game sends the ad partners both the US "do not sell or share" opt-out signal and the European "no consent" signal, so the ads you see are not personalised.
  • Tracking on iPhone: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: delete or reset it in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • No ads: a one-time purchase in Skins that turns off the ads between races; the Continue video stays optional. After a reinstall, Reset all data or on a new phone, use Restore purchases in Skins or Settings.
  • Report an ad: Settings → Privacy & legal → Report an ad (right below Personalised ads) opens your e-mail app with a report to us that already contains the ad network, the ad's creative id and the time of the last ad shown since you opened the game. Add what you saw and send it. You can also simply e-mail us with the time and, if you can, a screenshot.
  • A Continue video gave no reward? E-mail us with the time.

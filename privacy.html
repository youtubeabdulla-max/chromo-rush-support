Chromo Rush — Privacy Policy

Updated 25 October 2026 · effective 25 October 2026 · applies to Chromo Rush 2.1.0 and later, and to the earlier versions 2.0 and 1.1 (see "Changes to this policy")

Who we are

Chromo Rush is made and published by Abdulla Alhashmi, an independent game developer ("we", "us"). We decide how the data described in this policy is used and are responsible for it. You can reach us at youtubeabdulla@gmail.com with any question about this policy or about your data.

Summary

Chromo Rush is a game for players of all ages. It has no accounts, no analytics of its own and no game server, and we do not sell your data for money. Your game (your racers, stars, rainbows, stickers and settings) is stored only on your device. The first time you open the game it asks how old you are; only the answer's band (under 13, or 13 and over) is kept, on your device. Players under 13, and players whose age the game does not know yet, get only child-directed ads that are never personalised, and they are never asked to allow tracking. The game shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which fills them from our ad partners Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These partners process some device data to show and measure ads. For players 13 and over they are asked to personalise ads only if the player allows it (and, on iPhone, only if the player also allows tracking). You can buy racers, ALL 5 RACERS, SPECIAL TRAILS and No ads through the App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment; Unity IAP, the purchase software in the game, sends Unity a record of each purchase step, which on iPhone includes your device's identifier for vendor, and the Unity Ads software in the game can itself record your in-app purchases for Unity's advertising and analytics. See "Your age and children", "Advertising", "Purchases" and "Your ad choices".

What the app stores on your device

  • Your game: the racers you own and whether you own ALL 5 RACERS, SPECIAL TRAILS or No ads, the racer you use and what your ball wears (a trail, a trim, a colour of Sunny), your settings (music, sounds, reduced effects, large text, obstacle captions and Rival Race difficulty), your stars and best time on each stage of the Sky Path (and, per stage, how often you finished without first place, which makes that stage's rivals a little easier), the part of the map you looked at last, your best Rival Race times and the counters that move its Auto difficulty, how many races you have finished and your first Rival Races, whether you have played the how-to race, and which version of the Terms you accepted.
  • Your rainbow: the rainbow paint your races have painted, the prizes (trails and trims) you have won and the last rainbow card shown. Your stickers and the colours of Sunny are worked out from your stars; they are not stored separately.
  • The gift balloon: how many you have popped, and the day and time of day of your last one with the phone's running-clock reading at that moment, so that one balloon waits on each new day you play (nothing is lost on days you don't).
  • A try in progress (TRY: which racer and how many of its three races are left) and a "Keep" card still to show after it.
  • A purchase still waiting for a parent's approval (Ask to Buy, or a pending payment): which one and when it was asked, kept for a day at most, also through Reset all data, so the same thing is never offered twice while a parent decides.
  • Your answer to the age question HOW OLD ARE YOU?: only its band (under 13, or 13 and over), never your age itself; your answer to the "Personalised ads" question (13 and over); and the time the last full-screen ad started (to keep the ads apart, see "Advertising").
  • All of this lives in the app's private storage and never leaves the phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Privacy & legal → Reset all data (Reset all data keeps only a purchase still waiting for approval, as above).
  • If you played version 1.x: on its first start, the update from version 1.x read your 1.x save to carry over your skins, No ads, settings, best times and ad choice, and it keeps a list of what it gave you for its one-time thank-you card. That old save (your 1.x progress, racer name and a random guest identifier) stays on the device unchanged (the game reads it again only if its own save can no longer be read); the game never sends it anywhere, and Reset all data or uninstalling deletes it.
  • What the update from version 1.x gave you (the thank-you skin Prism, and the skins and No ads it gave you for what you owned or had saved up in version 1.x) lives only on this device: deleting the game or Reset all data removes it, and Restore purchases cannot bring it back, because it was never a store purchase (only your phone's own backup, described below, may hold a copy). The same is true of everything the game gives for free: rainbow prizes, stickers, colours of Sunny and tries.
  • The ad partners' software and Unity IAP keep their own files in the app's storage (on iPhone, for example, Unity IAP keeps a list of the App Store transactions it has already handled). Reset all data does not touch those files; uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Like other apps' data, the game's data can be part of your phone's own backup (iCloud, or Android's backup to your Google account) if you have turned it on. Apple or Google keep that backup for you, and it can put the game's data back on a restored or new phone (on Android, also when you reinstall the game). We have no access to it; you manage it in your phone's settings.

Single player

Chromo Rush is a single-player game: you race computer-controlled rivals on your own device. There is no online play, no chat, no player names and no game server; the game never sends your moves, stars or race results anywhere, and it sends no notifications. An ad never interrupts a race: during a race a video only plays if you tap to watch one (the Continue video in Rival Race), and the ad partners then receive the data described in "Advertising".

Your age and children

Chromo Rush's content is suitable for all ages (App Store 4+, Everyone / PEGI 3), and the game is made for everyone, children included. The first time you open it (and again after Reset all data, or if the game's own save can no longer be read), a welcome card asks HOW OLD ARE YOU?, with one button for each age from "under 5" to "18+" and none picked for you. The answer is used only to choose how ads are requested, and only its band is kept, on your device.

  • Under 13, or age not known. Until the age is answered, and for every player who says they are under 13, the game marks every ad request as child-directed: it sets the child-directed (COPPA) flag of Unity LevelPlay, the child-directed and under-age tags of Google AdMob and the child flag of Unity Ads, and asks the ad partners not to use the device's advertising identifier. The ads are then never personalised, the game never asks to allow tracking, and there is no "Personalised ads" question or switch. The ad partners still receive the device details, the approximate location from the IP address and the ad interactions described in "Advertising", which they need to deliver, count and protect child-directed ads, under their rules for children.
  • For every player, children or not, LevelPlay is asked to use only ad networks that are self-certified for Google Play's Families policy, and Google AdMob is asked to show only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game never asks for a name, an e-mail address, a photo or a location, and it has no chat and no user content. If a child e-mails us, we use the e-mail only to answer it and then delete it.
  • For parents. Every purchase needs the store's own confirmation, and the store's parental controls (Ask to Buy on iPhone, purchase approvals in Google Play or Family Link) apply in the game; a purchase waiting for approval shows WAITING. Settings → Privacy & legal → Reset all data erases the game's data on the device and asks the age question again. You can contact us about your child's data at any time (see "Your rights").

Advertising

Chromo Rush shows three kinds of ads:

  • The Continue video (Rival Race only) — when your ball bursts after you have passed at least two obstacles (not in your first, how-to race), the game may offer a short video, at most once per race. It plays only if you tap Continue, and it lets you carry on from just below the spot where your ball burst instead of going back to the last checkpoint. Closing it early simply gives no reward. Sky Path stages have no Continue video.
  • The TRY video — in Skins, beside the price of a racer you do not own, you may tap TRY to watch a short video and then race three races as that racer. It plays only if you tap it, and it never gives stars, paint, prizes or anything you keep.
  • Short ads between races — on the Sky Path, none until stage 3 has been raced to its flag; after that, a stage that ran to its flag can be followed by one when you start your next stage, at least 60 seconds after the last full-screen ad started (a short ad or a TRY video), and never at the start of a Summit, after a new obstacle's start sheet or from the new-sky card. In Rival Race, after a race that ran to its end (a race you quit does not count), one may show when you start your next Rival Race, at least 45 seconds after the last one started, and none follows a race in which a Continue video was shown. None is shown when you open the game, during a race or on the way to a result, and none once you own No ads.

The ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay, the ad mediation service of Unity Technologies (formerly ironSource). For each ad, LevelPlay picks an ad from one of our ad partners: Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) or Google AdMob (Google). Their software in the app collects the data below and sends it to them; we do not receive it. They use it under their own privacy policies:

  • Advertising identifier and other device identifiers: for players 13 and over, on Android the Google advertising ID (unless you have deleted it in Android settings), and on iPhone Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking; and other identifiers that the ad partners' software can read on any device, such as the identifier for vendor (IDFV) on iPhone and the app set ID on Android. For a player under 13 or of unknown age, the game asks the ad partners not to use the advertising identifier (see "Your age and children").
  • Device information: device model and maker, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier, and the app's name and version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, watched to the end, skipped or tapped, and whether an advertised app was later installed.
  • Approximate location: estimated from your IP address (roughly country or city). The game never uses GPS and never asks for location permission.
  • Diagnostic data about the ad software, such as load times, errors and crashes.
  • In-app purchases (Unity Ads only): the Unity Ads software in the game can itself record the in-app purchases made on your device, such as the item, its price and the store's transaction details, and Unity uses them for advertising and analytics under its privacy policy (linked below). Unity decides whether this recording runs. The game's own code does not send your purchases to the ad partners, and we do not receive this data.

The ad partners use this data to deliver ads, to choose which ads to show, to cap how often you see the same ad, to measure how ads perform (which is how we are paid), and to detect fraud and invalid traffic. If personalised ads are not allowed (a player under 13, a player whose age is not known yet, or the answer No thanks), we tell them so and they show only non-personalised ads, chosen from context such as the game and your approximate location rather than from a profile of you; they still process the data above to deliver, cap, measure and protect the ads. Their policies: Unity (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads), Google and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Apart from Unity IAP's purchase records (see "Purchases"), we share data only with these ad partners, only through their software in the app and only for the purposes described here, and we never sell it. The game's own code does not tell the ad partners what you bought, but the Unity Ads software can record your in-app purchases itself (see "In-app purchases" above). Each ad partner is bound by its own privacy policy, its agreement with us and the App Store and Google Play rules to protect that data at least as well as this policy describes.

Ads need an internet connection; the races themselves work offline. If an ad cannot be loaded, you simply keep playing. We do not choose the individual ads that the partners show; if you see an ad that seems inappropriate, please report it (see "Reporting an ad").

Purchases

Chromo Rush is free to play. In Skins (tap your ball on Home) you can buy racers, one by one or all five together as ALL 5 RACERS; SPECIAL TRAILS, six trails behind your ball; and No ads, which stops the ads between races (the Continue and TRY videos stay something you choose to watch). Racers and trails change only how your ball and the course look, never how a race plays, and no purchase gives stars, rainbow paint, prizes or stickers. Each is a one-time purchase, and the store shows its price before you buy.

  • Payment. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment details, and we receive no name or e-mail address from the store.
  • Unity IAP. The game uses Unity IAP, from Unity Technologies, to run purchases. On each purchase step (starting a purchase, paying, a failure, the confirmed purchase) Unity IAP sends Unity a record of it: the item (its store id, title, description and type), its price and currency, the store's transaction id and, once paid, the store's receipt, on iPhone the identifier for vendor (IDFV), the device model, operating system version, language and region settings, when the device was last started and its total storage, and the app's id, version and install date (on Android also how the app was installed). Because the records from an iPhone carry its IDFV, they can be linked to that device. The records contain no name, e-mail address or payment details and no advertising identifier, and Unity IAP sends them only to Unity; it does not pass them to the ad partners' software in the game (the Unity Ads software's own purchase records are described below). Unity uses them as analytics for its purchase service; we do not receive them, and they are part of Unity IAP, so the game cannot switch them off. Unity is bound by its own privacy policy, its terms with us and the App Store and Google Play rules to protect these records at least as well as this policy describes. Unity's privacy policies, which also tell you how to contact Unity about these records: for players of games that use Unity's services and Unity's general privacy policy.
  • Unity Ads. Separately from Unity IAP, the Unity Ads software in the game (one of our ad partners, see "Advertising") can itself record the in-app purchases made on your device, such as the item, its price and the store's transaction details. Unity uses them for advertising and analytics under its privacy policies (linked above). Unity decides whether this recording runs. The game's own code does not send your purchases to the ad partners, and we do not receive this data.
  • What our store privacy details say. Our App Store privacy details list these purchase records as Purchase History, linked to you and not used for tracking, for analytics (Unity IAP and Unity Ads) and third-party advertising (Unity Ads). Our Google Play Data safety section lists them as purchase history, collected and shared with Unity (Unity IAP and Unity Ads), for analytics and for advertising or marketing (Unity Ads).
  • What the game keeps. What you own is stored only on your device (see "What the app stores on your device"); we have no server and keep no copy. A purchase waiting for a parent's approval is kept for a day at most, so that neither it nor something it includes (a racer of ALL 5 RACERS) is offered again while a parent decides; it arrives once the purchase is approved.
  • Restore purchases. Restore purchases, in Skins and in Settings, asks the App Store or Google Play which racers, ALL 5 RACERS, SPECIAL TRAILS and No ads you bought and brings them back, for example after a reinstall, after Reset all data or on a new device signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account. The game also asks the store by itself each time it starts (and on Android each time it comes back to the front), so what you bought usually comes back without Restore purchases. What the update from version 1.x gave you, and what the game gives for free, is not a store purchase, so Restore purchases cannot bring it back.
  • Refunds and purchase history are handled by Apple or Google; they keep your purchase history under their own privacy policies.

Your ad choices

  • Personalised ads (13 and over). After the age question, a player 13 or over is asked once whether ads may be personalised: No thanks or Yes, personalise; if you answered in version 1.x or 2.0, that answer carries over. The game plays the same whatever you answer, you see the same number of ads, and neither answer earns a reward; with No thanks you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your answer, and so withdraw your consent, at any time with the Personalised ads switch in Settings → Privacy & legal; it applies to the ads loaded after the change. A player under 13 is not asked: their ads are never personalised.
  • Tracking on iPhone (13 and over). After that question, iOS asks once whether Chromo Rush may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the ad partners cannot use Apple's advertising identifier. You can change it at any time in the iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. A player under 13 is never asked.
  • Android advertising ID. You can delete or reset it in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • No ads. A one-time purchase in Skins (or the NO ADS button on Home) that switches off the ads between races. The Continue and TRY videos stay available, but they only ever play when you choose to watch one. If you bought No ads, Restore purchases brings it back after Reset all data or a reinstall; No ads that the update from version 1.x gave you lives only on that device.
  • Data the ad partners hold. We cannot see or delete it. To access or delete it, contact Unity or Google through the policies linked above; they recognise you by your advertising identifier. For the purchase records of Unity IAP and Unity Ads, contact Unity through its privacy policies (see "Purchases").
  • US state privacy laws. Some US state laws call giving ad partners your data for personalised ads a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. When you answer No thanks, or turn the Personalised ads switch off, the game sends the ad partners (through Unity LevelPlay) the US "do not sell or share my personal information" opt-out signal together with the European "no consent to personalised ads" signal. You still see ads, but they are not personalised. When you answer Yes, personalise, or turn the switch on, the game sends consent and no opt-out instead. For a player under 13 or of unknown age, the game always sends no consent and the opt-out, with the child-directed flags. Your current choice is sent at every launch before any ad loads, and again whenever you change it.
  • Report an ad. Settings → Privacy & legal → Report an ad, or e-mail us; see "Reporting an ad" below.
  • Everything on your device. Use Settings → Privacy & legal → Reset all data to erase your racers, No ads, settings, stars, best times, rainbows, prizes, stickers, age answer and ad choice from this device (a purchase still waiting for a parent's approval keeps waiting), or uninstall the app to remove everything, including the files that the ad partners' software and Unity IAP keep in the app. What you bought comes back by itself, or with Restore purchases.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, misleading or broken, use Settings → Privacy & legal → Report an ad. It opens your e-mail app with a report to youtubeabdulla@gmail.com that already contains the details of the last ad shown since you opened the game (a Continue or TRY video or an ad between races, the ad network, the ad's creative id, the ad unit and the time), the app version and your device's operating system; if no ad has been shown since you opened the game, the report says so. Add what you saw (a screenshot helps) and send it; nothing is sent until you send the e-mail yourself. You can also simply e-mail that address with the game's name, the time and, if you can, a screenshot. We use a report, and the e-mail address it comes from, only to look into the ad (for example to have it blocked through the ad partner) and to reply to you.

How long data is kept

  • Game data on your device is kept until you use Settings → Privacy & legal → Reset all data or uninstall the app; a purchase waiting for a parent's approval is kept for a day at most. We do not have a copy: there is no game server.
  • Ad reports and support e-mails, with the e-mail address they come from, are kept only as long as we need them to deal with your message and are deleted within 12 months; you can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.
  • The ad partners keep the data they collect for the periods set out in their own privacy policies (linked above), and you can ask them to delete it.
  • Unity keeps the purchase records sent by Unity IAP or recorded by Unity Ads, and Apple and Google keep your purchase history, for the periods set out in their own privacy policies.

Keeping data safe

We run no game server, and the game itself sends us nothing. The game's data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read (and, if you turned it on, in your phone's own backup). The ad partners' software and Unity IAP send their data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The only personal data we hold is an e-mail you choose to send us (an ad report, which the game fills in for you, or a support request); we keep it in our own e-mail account, use it only to deal with your message, and delete it as described in "How long data is kept".

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent to personalised ads at any time (Settings → Privacy & legal → Personalised ads, and on iPhone the tracking setting), erase the game's data with Reset all data, and ask us at any time what data we hold about you or your child (normally only an e-mail you sent us), or to correct or delete it: e-mail youtubeabdulla@gmail.com. We answer within one month. For the data held by the ad partners, or by Unity for the purchase records of Unity IAP and Unity Ads, contact them through the policies linked above. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Permissions

The game uses network access only for ads and purchases. The Android version declares the permissions that the game engine, the ad partners' software and Google Play's purchase software need (such as network access, the advertising ID, Android's ad-services permissions, basic phone state such as the type of mobile network, short background work, and the Google Play billing permission); none of them asks you for anything. On iPhone the game asks a player 13 or over once for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency); it never asks a player under 13. No camera, microphone, contacts, device location or photo access is used, and the game sends no notifications.

Changes to this policy

When the app changes how it handles data, we update this page before that version is released; the date at the top shows the latest change. This version was written for Chromo Rush 2.1.0, which made the game for all ages (the age question and child-directed ads for players under 13), added the Sky Path's stages and stars, rainbows and their prizes, stickers, the gift balloon, the TRY video, ALL 5 RACERS and SPECIAL TRAILS, and keeps all of it only on your device. If you still have version 2.0 installed, it uses the same ad partners, purchases (without ALL 5 RACERS and SPECIAL TRAILS), Unity IAP records and ad choices as described above, has no age question, and treats every player as 13 or over. If you still have version 1.1 installed: it has no store purchases (so Unity IAP sends nothing from it), keeps its progress only on your device, and uses the same ad partners, ad choices and Report an ad as described above (in 1.1 they are in Settings).
